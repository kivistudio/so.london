Sea beech, labelled *Delesseria sanguinea* by Anna Atkins and still known by that name; her label adds "(much cover'd with corallines)". From *Photographs of British Algae: Cyanotype Impressions* (1843–53), Part V. Reproduced from the Spencer Collection copy, from The New York Public Library.

Open edition giclée print on Hahnemühle Bamboo, A3, in a black wood frame without a mount. Printed by theprintspace in London.
